# Vocabulary
E|What does "enormous" mean?|very large|very small|very fast|very quiet|"Enormous" means extremely large in size or amount.
E|Choose the word that means "happy".|cheerful|gloomy|angry|tired|"Cheerful" describes someone who is noticeably happy and positive.
E|Which word means "to begin"?|start|finish|stop|pause|"Start" and "begin" are synonyms meaning to set something in motion.
E|What is the opposite of "ancient"?|modern|old|historic|aged|"Ancient" means very old; "modern" means belonging to the present time.
E|Fill in the blank: Please ___ the door; it's cold outside.|close|open|break|paint|Closing the door keeps the cold out; the context makes "close" the only logical choice.
E|Which word describes someone who is not afraid?|brave|timid|nervous|shy|"Brave" means ready to face danger or difficulty without fear.
E|What does "tiny" mean?|very small|very large|very loud|very bright|"Tiny" is an informal word meaning extremely small.
E|Choose the word that means "a place where books are kept and borrowed".|library|bakery|garage|pharmacy|A library is a building or room containing collections of books for people to read or borrow.
E|Which word means "quick"?|rapid|slow|lazy|calm|"Rapid" means happening in a short time or at great speed.
E|What is a "meal eaten in the morning" called?|breakfast|dinner|supper|dessert|Breakfast is the first meal of the day, eaten in the morning.
E|Fill in the blank: The baby is ___ because she is hungry.|crying|laughing|sleeping|dancing|A hungry baby typically cries; the other options do not fit the cause given.
E|Which word means "to fix something that is broken"?|repair|destroy|lose|ignore|"Repair" means to restore something damaged to good condition.
E|What does "polite" mean?|having good manners|being very loud|being very rich|being very tall|A polite person shows respect and good manners toward others.
E|Which word means "a person who teaches"?|teacher|student|driver|farmer|A teacher is someone whose job is to teach, especially in a school.
E|Choose the word that means "difficult".|hard|easy|simple|soft|"Hard" can mean "difficult", as in "a hard question".
E|What is the opposite of "empty"?|full|hollow|bare|vacant|"Full" means containing as much as possible; "empty" means containing nothing.
E|Fill in the blank: I need an ___ because it is raining.|umbrella|oven|eraser|envelope|An umbrella protects you from rain.
E|Which word means "to speak very quietly"?|whisper|shout|scream|yell|To whisper is to speak using your breath rather than your voice, very softly.
